Where your pace

Elite fast bowling has been measured in laboratories for decades, with eighteen cameras and markers taped to the body. Film one delivery from the side on a phone and this returns the same twelve measurements, set against the elite reference values.

Filming the delivery

The measurement is only as good as the angle you shoot from. Five things matter; the rest does not.

  • Stand square to the bowlerSide on, level with the crease, about 8–10 m away. Not behind, not at an angle.
  • Both feet and the head in frameThrough the whole delivery stride. If the feet leave the frame the stride cannot be measured.
  • 120 frames per secondSlow-motion mode on any recent phone. At 30 fps the foot contacts fall between frames.
  • Hold the phone stillBrace it or use a tripod. Panning moves the background the model uses for scale.
  • One delivery per clipFrom run-up to follow-through. Three to five seconds is plenty.
  • Send six, not oneUpload several deliveries together and the report leads with their average. A single clip carries event-detection noise that largely cancels over six.

Analyse your deliveries

One clip works. Several clips of the same bowler work better: the report averages them and leaves out any value that looks like a detection error.
